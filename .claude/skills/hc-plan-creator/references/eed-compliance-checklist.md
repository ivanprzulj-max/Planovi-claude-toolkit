# EED compliance checklist for local H&C plans

Checklist for cross-checking a municipal Heating & Cooling plan against the recast Energy Efficiency Directive (EU/2023/1791), Article 25 (heating and cooling assessment and planning) and the related Annex X content requirements, as operationalised in the ESCALATE methodology.

**Important**: this checklist is a working aid, not the legal text. When the directive text, the ESCALATE guidelines, or national transposition documents are available in the Project, verify against those primary sources — they prevail over this file. If you find this checklist diverging from the primary sources, flag it to the user so the checklist can be updated.

## A. Demand side

- [ ] Mapping of current heating and cooling demand by sector (residential, services/public, industry), with reference year stated
- [ ] Spatial dimension of demand (zones/mapping consistent with available GIS layers, spatial plans, cadastres)
- [ ] Building stock characterisation relevant to H&C demand (age, renovation status, energy performance where data exist)
- [ ] Demand projections to the plan's milestone years (typically 2030/2035/2040/2045/2050), with methodology and assumptions stated
- [ ] Energy-efficiency/renovation potential reflected in demand trajectories

## B. Supply side

- [ ] Inventory of current H&C supply: technologies, fuels, capacities, district heating (CTS) systems where present
- [ ] Assessment of renewable energy potentials for H&C (geothermal, solar thermal, biomass, heat pumps/ambient energy, etc.)
- [ ] Assessment of **waste heat** potentials (industry, wastewater, data centres, other sources per EED Art. 25/26 scope)
- [ ] Potential for efficient district heating and cooling ("učinkovito centralizirano grijanje i hlađenje") assessed against the EED Article 26 definition and its milestone-year thresholds
- [ ] Cogeneration (high-efficiency CHP) potential where relevant

## C. Scenarios and trajectories

- [ ] At least a reference/baseline scenario and one or more decarbonisation scenarios to the milestone years
- [ ] Scenario methodology and key assumptions documented (delegate the analytical work to the hc-scenario-analysis skill; this checklist verifies the plan documents it properly)
- [ ] Least-cost / techno-economic comparison of supply options (LCOH/LCOE) present and traceable to the Master Sheet
- [ ] Compliance trajectory for efficient-DHC status (EED Art. 26) shown for CTS systems, per milestone year

## D. Grid and infrastructure (REGEA addition)

- [ ] Electricity grid analysis sections present per the REGEA-extended ESCALATE template (added at cities' request; part of the canonical ToC even though beyond the base template)
- [ ] Interaction of electrification of heat (heat pumps) with grid capacity addressed

## E. Policy, governance, implementation

- [ ] Policy measures identified, linked to the scenarios they enable
- [ ] Responsible actors and indicative timeline for measures
- [ ] Financing considerations / funding sources indicated
- [ ] Stakeholder involvement documented: who was consulted, how, and how input was reflected (EED emphasises participation of relevant local stakeholders)
- [ ] Alignment with national and local strategies (NECP, national energy/climate strategies, spatial plans) — cited with document names and dates
- [ ] Monitoring/review provisions: how and when the plan will be updated

## F. Formal/document quality

- [ ] Plan in Croatian, formal register, EED terminology used consistently
- [ ] Every figure traceable to the Master Sheet or a cited, dated source (no orphan numbers)
- [ ] Measured data / modelled estimates / assumptions clearly distinguished
- [ ] Open items (missing data) listed explicitly for the city
- [ ] Changelog section (Evidencija izmjena) present and current
- [ ] ToC conforms to the Karlovac-based canonical template

## Reporting gaps

For every unchecked item, record: the missing element, the EED provision or template section it maps to, why it is missing (data unavailable / not yet drafted / pending city input), and the proposed resolution. Gaps are reported openly — never papered over with generic text.
